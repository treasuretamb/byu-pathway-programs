What You'll Study

The 4 Courses in the Entrepreneurship Certificate

Each course mirrors a real stage of business development. By the end you'll have a validated business concept and the skills to execute it.

BUS 116
Starting a Business
Explore what it means to be an entrepreneur. Complete a feasibility analysis, validate your business idea, and learn the mindset and habits that separate successful founders from the rest.
BUS 117
Growing a Business
Take a validated concept and build it. Marketing strategy, customer acquisition, managing early growth, and the financial fundamentals every growing business needs.
BUS 118
Expanding a Business
Scale up. Learn to expand into new markets, manage a growing team, navigate partnerships, and structure your business for sustainable expansion.
BUS 119
Strengthening a Business
Build resilience. Manage risk, navigate economic challenges, maintain profitability under pressure, and position your business for long-term strength.

What You'll Be Able To Do

Skills That Turn Ideas Into Income

Entrepreneurship is the most transferable skill there is. These are the competencies every founder and every employer of founders looks for.

Ideation

Validate a business idea before investing time or money using real market research and feasibility frameworks.

Business Planning

Write a real business plan with financial projections, market analysis, and a clear value proposition.

Marketing

Attract and retain customers with a strategy grounded in your target audience, competitive positioning, and budget.

Finance

Read and manage the numbers, revenue, expenses, cash flow, and profitability at every stage of growth.

Leadership

Build and lead a team, delegate effectively, and create a culture that keeps your best people engaged.

Resilience

Navigate the inevitable challenges of running a business, economic downturns, competition, and operational setbacks.

Getting Started

Three Steps to Enrol

No business experience required, just a genuine desire to build something and the discipline to see it through.

Step 1

Start PathwayConnect

Complete the one semester PathwayConnect foundation program. Fully online and affordable worldwide.

Step 2

Apply to BYU-Idaho

Once PathwayConnect is complete, apply to BYU-Idaho to begin your Entrepreneurship certificate courses and start your Business Management degree pathway.

Step 3

Start Building

Begin with Starting a Business (BUS 116) and work through each stage sequentially. By the end of the certificate you'll have a validated business concept ready to launch.
