What You'll Study

The 7 Courses in the MBC Certificate

Courses must be taken in sequence, each one builds directly on the last, from anatomy to coding to billing.

MCO 180
Introduction to Medical Billing & Coding (Block 1)
An overview of the healthcare industry, medical terminology, coding systems (ICD, CPT, HCPCS), and the billing cycle. The foundation that every subsequent course builds on.
MCO 210
Body Systems 1 (Block 2)
Anatomy and medical terminology for the musculoskeletal, integumentary, and nervous systems, the first group of body systems you'll need to code accurately.
MCO 211
Body Systems 2 (Block 2, with MCO 210)
Anatomy and terminology for the cardiovascular, respiratory, and digestive systems taken alongside Body Systems 1 in the same block.
MCO 212
Body Systems 3 (Block 3)
Anatomy and terminology for the urinary, reproductive, endocrine, and sensory systems completing your full body systems knowledge base.
MCO 220
Medical Coding 1 (Block 3, with MCO 212)
Apply ICD and CPT coding to real medical records. Practice assigning diagnosis and procedure codes accurately for common conditions and treatments.
MCO 221
Medical Coding 2 (Block 4)
Advanced coding for surgical procedures, specialty practices, and complex cases. Builds speed and accuracy on the coding skills needed for professional certification.
MCO 222
Medical Coding 3 (Block 4, with MCO 221)
The billing side claim submission, insurance follow up, payment posting, denial management, and compliance. How the coding work translates into revenue for healthcare providers.

What You'll Be Able To Do

Skills in Constant Demand Across Healthcare

Every hospital, clinic, and health insurer needs accurate medical coding to function. These are the skills that make you essential in any healthcare administration environment.

Medical Coding

Assign accurate ICD-10 and CPT codes to diagnoses and procedures, the core technical skill that every medical billing role requires.

Anatomy

Understand the body systems well enough to interpret clinical documentation and assign codes correctly without a medical degree.

Medical Terminology

Read and interpret clinical notes, operative reports, and discharge summaries written in medical language with confidence and accuracy.

Billing

Submit clean insurance claims, manage denials, post payments, and follow up on outstanding balances, the revenue cycle skills healthcare providers depend on.

Compliance

Apply HIPAA regulations and coding compliance standards protecting patient data and ensuring billing accuracy in a regulated environment.

Accuracy

Code with precision, errors in medical coding can delay payments, trigger audits, or cause legal issues. This certificate trains you to get it right.

Getting Started

Three Steps to Enrol

No medical background required, just attention to detail, a willingness to learn new terminology, and a desire to work in healthcare.

Step 1

Start PathwayConnect

Complete the one-semester PathwayConnect foundation program. Fully online and affordable. No prior healthcare knowledge or qualifications are required to begin.

Step 2

Apply to Ensign College

Once PathwayConnect is complete, apply to Ensign College. Note that MBC courses must be taken in a specific block sequence, your academic advisor will help you plan it correctly.

Step 3

Start with MCO 180

Begin with Introduction to Medical Billing & Coding in block 1. This course sets up everything that follows, take it first and the rest of the certificate falls into place naturally.
