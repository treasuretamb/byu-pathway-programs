What You'll Study

The 5 Courses in the TEFL Certificate

From foundational teaching methods to classroom practice, five courses that build you into a confident, qualified English language teacher.

TESOL 101
Introduction to TESOL
The foundation of language teaching, how people learn languages, the history and principles of TESOL methodology, lesson planning basics, and an introduction to the professional landscape of English language instruction worldwide.
TESOL 102
TESOL Pedagogy I
Classroom management, teaching listening and speaking skills, designing communicative activities, and building student confidence in spoken English. The most practical course in the certificate for face-to-face teaching.
TESOL 103
TESOL Pedagogy II
Teaching reading and writing in English, strategies for developing literacy, working with diverse proficiency levels, integrating technology into the classroom, and assessing student progress effectively.
TESOL 104
English Grammar for TESOL
A deep dive into English grammar from a teaching perspective not just what the rules are, but how to explain them to non native speakers clearly and confidently. Covers syntax, morphology, tense, aspect, and common learner errors.
TESOL 105
TESOL Practice
Put it all together. Supervised teaching practice where you design and deliver real lessons, receive feedback, and refine your teaching approach. The capstone experience of the certificate.

What You'll Be Able To Do

Skills That Travel Anywhere in the World

English is the global language of business, education, and opportunity. The ability to teach it is one of the most portable and in demand qualifications a person can hold.

Lesson Planning

Design structured, engaging lessons for different age groups and proficiency levels, from complete beginners to advanced business English students.

Speaking & Listening

Teach conversational English effectively building student confidence in spoken communication, pronunciation, and listening comprehension.

Reading & Writing

Develop student literacy in English, from basic reading skills to academic and professional writing appropriate for each learner's goals.

Grammar Instruction

Explain English grammar rules clearly to non native speakers covering the structures that learners find most difficult and most important.

Classroom Management

Run a productive classroom environment managing diverse learners, maintaining engagement, and adapting your approach when things aren't working.

Assessment

Evaluate student progress fairly and accurately using formative and summative assessment to guide instruction and demonstrate learning outcomes.

Getting Started

Three Steps to Enrol

If you're a confident English speaker who enjoys helping others, this certificate turns that into a professional qualification recognised worldwide.

Step 1

Start PathwayConnect

Complete the one-semester PathwayConnect foundation program. Fully online and affordable. The communication and academic writing skills you build in PathwayConnect directly support your TEFL coursework.

Step 2

Apply to BYU-Idaho

Once PathwayConnect is complete, apply to BYU-Idaho to begin your TEFL certificate courses and officially start your Applied Business Management degree pathway.

Step 3

Start Teaching

Begin with Introduction to TESOL and work through the pedagogy courses in sequence. The final TESOL Practice course gives you real supervised classroom experience before you graduate.
