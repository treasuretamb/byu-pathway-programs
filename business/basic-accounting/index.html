What You'll Study

The 5 Courses in the Basic Accounting Certificate

Practical, software based courses that give you skills you can use in any business from day one.

BUSPC 105
Spreadsheet Essentials
Build professional spreadsheets using advanced Excel functionality. Apply real life business data to visualise problems, track budgets, and present financial information clearly.
ACC 101
Financial Accounting I
The fundamentals of accounting, recording transactions, understanding the accounting equation, and producing the financial statements every business needs.
COMM 122
Interpersonal Communications
Communicate clearly and professionally in workplace settings through written, verbal, and interpersonal channels. A skill that makes every other skill more effective.
FIN 102
Personal Finance
Budgeting, saving, investing, insurance, and tax basics, personal finance skills that benefit you professionally and personally for life.
ACC 208
QuickBooks Online
Hands on training in QuickBooks Online, the world's most widely used accounting software. Set up accounts, record transactions, and produce reports employers expect.

What You'll Be Able To Do

Skills That Every Business Needs

Financial literacy is one of the most universally valued skills in the job market. These are the practical competencies this certificate builds.

Spreadsheets

Create professional Excel spreadsheets with formulas, charts, and advanced functions to analyse and present real business data.

Accounting

Record financial transactions correctly, understand debits and credits, and produce the three core financial statements every business runs on.

QuickBooks

Operate QuickBooks Online confidently, the tool used by millions of small and medium businesses worldwide to manage their finances.

Personal Finance

Apply sound financial principles to your own life through budgeting, managing debt, saving, and planning for the future with confidence.

Communication

Communicate financial information clearly and professionally in writing, in meetings, and in one on one conversations with colleagues and clients.

Analysis

Interpret financial data to identify trends, flag issues, and support decisions, a skill that gets you promoted faster than almost anything else.

Getting Started

Three Steps to Enrol

No prior accounting knowledge required, just a laptop that can run Microsoft Office and a willingness to learn the language of business.

Step 1

Start PathwayConnect

Complete the one semester PathwayConnect foundation program. You'll begin building spreadsheet skills in block 3 of PathwayConnect, giving you a head start before your certificate starts.

Step 2

Apply to Ensign College

Once PathwayConnect is complete, apply to Ensign College to begin your Basic Accounting certificate and officially start your Applied Business Management degree pathway.

Step 3

Start Counting

Begin with Spreadsheet Essentials and Financial Accounting I. By the end of the certificate you'll be QuickBooks certified and ready for real accounting roles.
