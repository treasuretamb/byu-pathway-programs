What You'll Study

The 4 Courses in the Administrative Assistant Certificate

Every course builds directly employable skills. By the end you'll be proficient in the tools and procedures that run every modern office.

BUS 105
Spreadsheet Essentials
Build professional Excel spreadsheets using advanced functionality, formulas, charts, data analysis, and visualisation. Apply these skills to real business scenarios throughout the course.
BUS 129
Office Procedures
Time management, interpersonal communications, records management, teamwork, customer service, meeting planning, and critical decision-making. The full toolkit of professional office life.
BUS 240
Word Processing I
Microsoft Word from top to bottom, document formatting, tables, graphics, macros, mail merge, styles, forms, and the professional document standards that make a real difference in any office.
BUS 180
Introduction to Financial Management
The financial basics every professional needs like budgeting, cash flow, reading financial reports, and applying financial thinking to everyday business decisions.

Getting Started

Three Steps to Enrol

No office experience required, just a laptop that runs Microsoft Office and a commitment to showing up professionally every day.

Step 1

Start PathwayConnect

Complete the one semester PathwayConnect foundation program. Fully online and affordable worldwide.

Step 2

Apply to BYU-Idaho

Once PathwayConnect is complete, apply to BYU-Idaho to begin your Administrative Assistant certificate courses and start your Business Management degree pathway.

Step 3

Start Working

Begin with Spreadsheet Essentials (BUS 105) and Office Procedures (BUS 129). These two courses alone will qualify you for many entry level office roles while you complete the rest of the certificate.
