What You'll Study

The 4 Courses in the Commercial Fundamentals Certificate

Four courses, four essential business competencies, each one directly applicable to real roles from day one.

BUS 105
Spreadsheet Essentials
Build professional spreadsheets using advanced Excel functionality. Apply real business data to visualise financial information, track performance, and present results clearly to decision makers.
COMM 150
Interpersonal Theory and Practice
Communication is the most universal business skill. This course builds persuasive, collaborative, and negotiation skills, the kind that improve every other competency you have.
MKT 120
Marketing Foundations
Understand how businesses attract and retain customers. Learn market analysis, target audiences, promotional strategy, pricing, and how to measure whether your marketing is working.
BUS 180
Introduction to Financial Management
The financial basics every business professional needs, budgeting, reading financial statements, managing cash flow, and using financial data to make smarter business decisions.

What You'll Be Able To Do

Skills That Work Across Every Industry

Commercial Fundamentals is BYU-Pathway's recommended business start because its skills are broad enough to open doors in almost any sector and specific enough to be immediately useful.

Spreadsheets

Create and manage Excel spreadsheets that track business performance, model scenarios, and communicate results to managers and clients.

Communication

Persuade, negotiate, and collaborate effectively building communication skills that determine how far and how fast you progress in any role.

Marketing

Analyse markets, design campaigns, and measure results. Every business needs people who understand how to find and keep customers.

Finance

Read financial statements, manage budgets, monitor cash flow, and use financial data to make decisions that improve business outcomes.

Business Operations

Understand how a business works as a system connecting sales, marketing, finance, and operations into a coherent picture.

Analysis

Use data from spreadsheets, marketing campaigns, and financial reports to identify what's working, what isn't, and what to do about it.

Getting Started

Three Steps to Enrol

No prior business experience required, just a laptop that runs Microsoft Office and a willingness to develop skills that work everywhere.

Step 1

Start PathwayConnect

Complete the one semester PathwayConnect foundation program. Fully online and affordable. You'll begin building spreadsheet fundamentals in PathwayConnect that directly support this certificate.

Step 2

Apply to BYU-Idaho

Once PathwayConnect is complete, apply to BYU-Idaho to begin your Commercial Fundamentals certificate and officially start your Applied Business Management degree pathway.

Step 3

Start Building

Begin with Spreadsheet Essentials and Interpersonal Communication. In just 12 credits you'll have a business skill set that works across sales, marketing, operations, and finance roles.
