What You'll Study

The 5 Courses in the HTM Certificate

Five courses that take you from the basics of hospitality all the way to financial management, covering every department you'll encounter in a real hotel, restaurant, or tourism operation.

BUS 113
Introduction to Hospitality & Tourism
The full landscape of the industry like hotels, food & beverage, travel agencies, events, and tourism. Learn the sectors, career pathways, and the professional and ethical standards that define them.
BUS 133
Teams & Relationships in Hospitality
Hospitality runs on people. Learn to build relationships with guests and colleagues, work effectively in teams, handle difficult situations professionally, and develop the service ethic that separates good hospitality from great hospitality.
BUS 153
Accommodations & Lodging Operations
How hotels actually work, front desk operations, housekeeping, occupancy management, property management systems, revenue reporting, and special events. Practical, operations level knowledge for anyone wanting to work in lodging.
BUS 233
Food & Beverage Industry
Catering, restaurant operations, corporate dining, front of house and back of house management, and inventory control. Food & beverage is one of the highest-employment areas in the entire hospitality sector.
BUS 243
Hospitality & Tourism Financial Management
The money side of hospitality, financial statement analysis, cost control, menu pricing, forecasting, budgeting, labour planning, and inventory control. Industry simulated assignments throughout make this immediately applicable.

What You'll Be Able To Do

Skills That the Hospitality Industry Demands

Tourism is one of Africa's fastest growing economic sectors. These are the practical skills that get you hired and promoted in it.

Guest Service

Deliver exceptional guest experiences, handle complaints, build loyalty, and maintain the service standards that define five-star hospitality.

Hotel Operations

Manage front desk, housekeeping, occupancy, and property systems, the operational backbone of any accommodation business.

Food & Beverage

Run a food and beverage operation from kitchen to table inventory, cost control, service standards, and supplier relationships.

Financial Management

Read P&L statements, control costs, price menus, forecast revenue, and manage budgets in a hospitality context.

Team Leadership

Lead service teams, coordinate across departments, and build the collaborative culture that keeps hospitality operations running smoothly under pressure.

Events

Plan and execute special events, conferences, and group bookings, a high value skill in both hotels and standalone event venues.

Getting Started

Three Steps to Enrol

Whether you're currently working in hospitality or just starting out, this certificate meets you where you are.

Step 1

Start PathwayConnect

Complete the one semester PathwayConnect foundation program. Fully online and affordable. No prior hospitality experience or qualifications are required to begin.

Step 2

Apply to Ensign College

Once PathwayConnect is complete, apply to Ensign College to begin your HTM certificate courses and officially start your Applied Business Management degree pathway.

Step 3

Start Serving

Begin with Introduction to Hospitality & Tourism and Teams & Relationships. You'll quickly build the skills and vocabulary that open doors in hotels, restaurants, and tourism operations.
