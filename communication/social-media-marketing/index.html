What You'll Study

The 5 Courses in the SMM Certificate

Every course is hands on, you'll leave with work you can actually show to employers.

DM 105
Introduction to Digital Marketing
The history and purpose of digital media, current platform trends, and how companies use digital marketing across the full customer journey.
DM 120
Digital Marketing Strategy
Set goals, identify target audiences, apply digital marketing tactics, and measure results. Build a complete strategy for a real brand.
DM 150
Digital Marketing Analytics
Capture and analyse data from online platforms using free and paid tools. Learn to make data driven decisions that actually move the needle.
DM 160
Digital Marketing Advertising
Create and run paid ad campaigns on Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, and more. Hands-on experience writing copy, designing creatives, and optimising for conversions.
SMM 130
Social Media Marketing Content
Content is the product. Learn to create copy, photos, videos, and infographics that drive real engagement across every major platform.

What You'll Be Able To Do

Skills That Employers Are Hiring For

Social media is one of the fastest-growing areas in marketing. These are the skills every employer in the space is looking for.

Content

Create written, visual, and video content tailored for each platform, from Instagram reels to LinkedIn articles.

Strategy

Build and execute a full digital marketing strategy from audience research to campaign execution and reporting.

Advertising

Design and run paid ad campaigns on Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube, and optimise them based on real data.

Analytics

Use analytics tools to track performance, interpret results, and make decisions backed by evidence not guesswork.

Brand Building

Build and grow a brand's online presence, community, and loyalty across multiple social media channels.

Community

Engage with audiences, handle customer communication, and manage brand reputation across platforms.

Getting Started

Three Steps to Enrol

You can start this process today, no prior experience in marketing required.

Step 1

Start PathwayConnect

Complete the one semester PathwayConnect foundation program. Affordable, fully online, and available worldwide. This certificate is available during PathwayConnect.

Step 2

Apply to Ensign College

Once PathwayConnect is complete, apply to Ensign College to begin your SMM certificate courses and officially start your Communication degree pathway.

Step 3

Start Creating

Register for your first semester and begin building the content, strategy, and analytics skills that employers are actively looking for right now.
