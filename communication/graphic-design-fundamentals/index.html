What You'll Study

The 4 Courses in the GDF Certificate

Every course builds a skill you'll use professionally and produces work you can put straight into your portfolio.

ART 125
Adobe CC Basics
A practical introduction to Illustrator, Photoshop, and InDesign, three core Adobe apps every designer must know. Beginner through intermediate features across all three tools.
ART 130
Introduction to Graphic Design
Explore contemporary visual communications like the principles, history, and practice of graphic design and related fields. Learn how design communicates beyond words.
ART 230
Typography I
Typography is the foundation of all design. Learn to set type with clear hierarchy, choose typefaces strategically, and use InDesign to produce publication ready text layouts.
ART 235
Graphic Design
Applied graphic design projects with an emphasis on creative, conceptual, and practical solutions. Deepen your Photoshop skills while building a portfolio of real design work.

What You'll Be Able To Do

Skills That Get You Hired

Visual communication is in demand across every industry. These are the skills employers and clients look for in a junior designer.

Adobe CC

Work confidently in Illustrator, Photoshop, and InDesign, the industry standard tools used in every professional design environment.

Typography

Set type with clear hierarchy, choose fonts with intention, and design layouts that guide the reader's eye and communicate the right message.

Visual Design

Apply the fundamental principles of design which iclude colour, contrast, alignment, repetition, and proximity to create work that actually works.

Conceptual Thinking

Move from brief to concept to final design following a process that produces consistent, effective results under real-world constraints.

Portfolio

Graduate with a portfolio of real design projects built during your coursework ready to show to employers and clients from day one.

Production

Prepare design files correctly for print and digital production, export settings, colour profiles, and handoff documentation done right.

Getting Started

Three Steps to Enrol

No art school required, just a commitment to learning the craft of visual design.

Step 1

Complete PathwayConnect

Unlike the SMM path, GDF is not available during PathwayConnect. You must first complete PathwayConnect and fully matriculate into BYU-Idaho.

Step 2

Matriculate into BYU-Idaho

After PathwayConnect, apply to BYU-Idaho. Once accepted and enrolled, you can register for your first GDF certificate courses.

Step 3

Start Designing

Begin with Adobe CC Basics and Introduction to Graphic Design. By the end of the certificate you'll have a working portfolio of real design projects.
