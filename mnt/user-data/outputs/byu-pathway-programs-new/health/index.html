What You'll Study

Subject Areas in the Health Analysis Certificate

Every course builds practical, employable skills in one of five key areas of public and community health.

COMMUNITY
Community & Environmental Health
Understand how environments — from homes and workplaces to entire communities — impact human health. Learn the basics of environmental health assessment and safety practices.
GLOBAL
Global Health
Examine health challenges at a global scale — how diseases spread, how interventions are designed, and how international organisations respond to health crises.
DISEASE
Human Disease
Learn to identify, describe, and understand the critical aspects of common diseases — including how they develop, spread, and are managed in community settings.
NUTRITION
Food & Nutrition
Apply the principles of food science and nutrition to support health and wellbeing across different populations and life stages.
DATA
Health Data & Spreadsheets
Develop introductory skills in organising, analysing, and interpreting health data using spreadsheet tools — a skill in demand across every area of public health.

Skills That Health Employers Look For

Public health is one of the fastest-growing sectors globally — especially across Africa. These are the foundation skills every entry-level health role requires.

🏥
Public Health

Apply health education and promotion principles to support individuals and communities in improving their health outcomes.

🦠
Disease Identification

Describe and differentiate common human diseases — essential for any role in community health, NGOs, or health departments.

🌍
Global Health

Understand evidence-based interventions for global health issues — and how to apply them in real communities.

📊
Data Management

Organise and interpret health data using spreadsheets — a cross-cutting skill demanded by every health employer.

🌿
Environmental Health

Explain environment-health relationships and describe environmental health and safety practices for your community.

💬
Communication

Communicate health information clearly to different audiences — from patients to policy makers to community groups.

Getting Started

Three Steps to Enrol

No health science background required — just a desire to improve lives in your community.

Step 1

Start PathwayConnect

Complete the one-semester PathwayConnect foundation program. Fully online and affordable. It builds the academic and life skills you'll need for your certificate courses.

Step 2

Apply to BYU-Idaho

Once PathwayConnect is complete, apply to BYU-Idaho to begin your Health Analysis certificate and officially start your Applied Health degree pathway.

Step 3

Start Serving

Begin your first health courses and start building the knowledge and skills that will let you make a real difference in the health of your community.
