What You'll Study

The 4 Courses in the TSE Certificate

Each course is practical and industry aligned preparing you for real support roles from day one.

CIT 101
IT Fundamentals
Hardware, software, operating systems, and basic networking, the foundation every IT professional needs.
CIT 111
Networking Fundamentals
How networks are built, managed, and secured. Covers TCP/IP, DNS, routers, and switches.
CIT 160
Introduction to Cybersecurity
Core security principles, threat types, and how to protect systems and data from real-world attacks.
CIT 201
Technical Support
Hands-on troubleshooting, customer communication, ticketing systems, and professional IT support workflows.

What You'll Be Able To Do

Skills That Employers Pay For

These aren't just academic outcomes, they're the same skills listed in real IT job postings worldwide.

Hardware

Diagnose and repair desktop, laptop, and peripheral hardware issues in professional environments.

Networking

Configure and troubleshoot wired and wireless networks using industry-standard protocols.

Security

Identify vulnerabilities, apply patches, and implement basic security policies to protect systems.

Support

Handle help desk tickets, communicate with end users, and resolve issues efficiently under pressure.

Operating Systems

Work confidently across Windows, macOS, and Linux environments, the three platforms every IT role requires.

Documentation

Write clear incident reports, knowledge base articles, and technical instructions that non-technical people can follow.

Getting Started

Three Steps to Enrol

The process is straightforward and you can start today.

Step 1

Start PathwayConnect

Complete the one-semester PathwayConnect foundation program. It's affordable, online, and available worldwide.

Step 2

Apply to Ensign College

Once PathwayConnect is complete, apply directly to Ensign College to begin your TSE certificate courses.

Step 3

Start Your Certificate

Register for your first semester of courses and access the Tech Lab at the Johannesburg GEC for in-person support.
