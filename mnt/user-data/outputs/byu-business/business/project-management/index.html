What You'll Study

The 5 Courses in the Project Management Certificate

Five practical courses that take you from project basics to advanced delivery — covering the methodologies used by teams worldwide.

PMPC 140
Intro to Project Management
The foundations: project lifecycle, scope, schedule, budget, risk, and stakeholder management. Learn the tools and vocabulary every project role requires.
CS 160
Agile Project Management
The modern approach. Learn Scrum, Kanban, and Agile principles — the methodology used by software teams, marketing departments, and operations teams worldwide.
SCM 125
Introduction to Supply Chain Management & Operations
Understand how organisations move goods, manage suppliers, and run efficient operations — essential context for any project manager in a physical or hybrid environment.
SCM 223
Quality Management
Deliver quality outcomes consistently. Learn Six Sigma basics, quality control frameworks, and the tools used to eliminate defects and improve processes.
PM 200
Advanced Project Management
Put it all together. Complex multi-phase projects, resource management, leadership under pressure, and the professional skills that advance careers in project delivery.

What You'll Be Able To Do

Skills That Every Organisation Needs

Project management is one of the most universally valued and transferable skill sets across every industry — technology, construction, healthcare, NGOs, and government.

Planning

Break any goal into a structured project plan with clear milestones, dependencies, budgets, and timelines.

Agile

Run Agile sprints using Scrum or Kanban — the frameworks used by the world's most productive teams.

Risk Management

Identify, assess, and mitigate project risks before they become problems that derail delivery.

Quality Control

Apply quality management frameworks to deliver consistent, measurable results that meet or exceed expectations.

Supply Chain

Understand how organisations source, move, and manage the physical inputs that projects depend on.

Leadership

Lead cross-functional teams, manage stakeholders, and keep projects on track through the inevitable complications.

Getting Started

Three Steps to Enrol

No prior project management experience required. If you've ever organised anything — an event, a team, a move — you already have the instincts. This certificate gives you the framework.

Step 1

Start PathwayConnect

Complete the one-semester PathwayConnect foundation program. Fully online and affordable worldwide.

Step 2

Apply to Ensign College

Once PathwayConnect is complete, apply to Ensign College to begin your Project Management certificate courses and start your Business Management degree pathway.

Step 3

Start Delivering

Begin with Intro to Project Management (PMPC 140) and Agile (CS 160). These two courses alone will qualify you for entry-level coordination roles.
